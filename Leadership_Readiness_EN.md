# Leadership_Readiness_EN

## Goal of the Tool

The goal of this tool is to support leaders' self-reflection in the areas of technical leadership, delivery, and team development.
The questions cover key areas that a modern leader should perceive: from strategy and technology through Agile practices to team culture and working with change. Tags under each question help identify key knowledge and skills that the question touches upon.
Each question is supplemented with example answers divided into TOP / MID / LOW categories.
These categories do not serve for quality assessment but help to better imagine different levels of depth or maturity of the answers.
Use this tool as a self-assessment – which questions seem easy to you and which make you think? What are your strengths? Where would you like to improve?
